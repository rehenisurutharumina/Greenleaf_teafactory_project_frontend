Factory Process

1

Receiving

Weigh and inspect fresh leaves.

2

Withering

Reduce moisture to make leaves soft.

3

Rolling

Crush and twist to release enzymes.

4

Oxidation

Develop color and aroma (for black tea).

5

Drying

Stop oxidation and preserve quality.

6

Sorting

Grade tea by size (OP/BOP/Dust).

7

Packing

Pack in moisture-proof bags for delivery.
