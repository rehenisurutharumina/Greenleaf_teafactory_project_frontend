How We Work

Our Production Process

From fresh leaf to finished product — every step is designed to ensure the highest quality tea.

01

Harvesting

Hand-picked tea leaves from the finest high-altitude plantations in Nuwara Eliya.

02

Withering & Rolling

Leaves are carefully withered to reduce moisture, then rolled to release essential oils.

03

Oxidation & Drying

Controlled oxidation develops the tea's flavor profile, followed by precise drying.

04

Grading & Packing

Expert sorting, quality grading, and moisture-proof packaging for global dispatch.
